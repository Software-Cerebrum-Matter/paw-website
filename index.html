Why microchipping & a licence matter

A walker is trusted with your dog outdoors. A microchip and licence are the best safety net if anything ever goes wrong.

🔎

Permanent ID

A microchip is a tiny chip placed under the skin by a vet. It can't fall off or be removed like a collar, so your dog can be identified for life.

📱

Faster return if lost

A vet or authority can scan the chip and use the registered details to contact you quickly, even if your dog slips its leash on a walk.

📄

Stay within the rules

The Greater Chennai Corporation requires pet dogs to be licensed, and owners without a licence can be fined (reported as ₹5,000). The pack keeps you covered.

💉

Vaccination records

Licensing goes with up-to-date anti-rabies vaccination, which protects your dog, your family and the dogs they meet in the park. Free anti-rabies shots are offered at Corporation clinics.

🏢

Peace of mind at home

Having your dog officially registered gives you paperwork to show when apartment associations or neighbours raise questions.

🤝

Trust both ways

You know your dog is identifiable; we know exactly who we're walking and who to call. It's how a professional walking service should work.

Rules, fees and deadlines can change. Check the latest on the Greater Chennai Corporation website; we'll confirm the current requirements with you at onboarding.

Questions

 

Do you only do dog walking?

Yes. We focus on walking so we can do it well, with a walker your dog knows and trusts.

Is the Onboarding Pack compulsory?

It's included for every new dog, because identification and paperwork are the foundation of safe walking. If your dog is already microchipped and licensed, we'll simply verify the details and adjust the pack.

What does microchipping involve?

A vet places a small chip under your dog's skin in a quick visit. We arrange the appointment, and your details are registered against the chip number.

Who pays the vet and licence fees?

Vet and government fees are paid at actual cost. We confirm every amount with you in advance.

What if it rains or it's very hot?

We schedule walks for cooler hours, and shorten or move them on extreme days. On rainy days we may do shorter walks or reschedule.

What if my dog is unwell or reactive?

Tell us at the meet and greet. We'll plan accordingly, or let you know honestly if a different service would suit your dog better.
