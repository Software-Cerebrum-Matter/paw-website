What we do for your dog

Three simple services, each built around your dog's routine, size and personality.

🦮

Dog Walking

Regular walks from a handler your dog gets to know and trust.

  • 30 or 60-minute walks
  • Early morning & evening slots, away from Chennai's midday heat
  • Water, waste pick-up & paw wipe-down
  • Walk report with photos
From ₹250 / walk
🏡

Dog Care at Home

Feeding, play and cuddles in your dog's own space while you're at work.

  • Feeding & fresh water
  • Playtime, training reinforcement
  • Medication & grooming basics
  • Cat-sitting add-on on request
From ₹350 / visit
🛏️

Boarding — Leave Your Dog With Us

Travelling or on holiday? Your dog stays in a clean, air-conditioned home setting, not a kennel.

  • Day care & overnight stays
  • Small groups, matched by size & temperament
  • Daily walks, meals & photo/video updates
  • 24×7 supervision, vet on call
From ₹700 / night

* Sample prices — final rates depend on dog size, location and duration.
